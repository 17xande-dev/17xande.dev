# Categories

Categories are how shoppers narrow the catalog: tick _Books_, and the catalog
shows only books. They are optional — a small shop can do without them — and a
product can be in as many as make sense.

## Add a category

1. Go to **Categories** and click **New category**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Name** — what shoppers read, such as _Books_.
   - **Slug** — the category's address, as in `/products?category=books`. Left
     blank, it is made from the name. Changing it later breaks links people have
     saved, and any link to it in your store's menu, so settle it early.
   - **Position** — the order categories are listed in. Lower numbers come
     first, so `0` is at the front. Leave gaps (10, 20, 30) and you can slot one
     in later without renumbering the rest.
3. Click **Save category**.

## Put products in it

Categories are chosen **on the product**, not on the category: open a product,
tick its categories under **Categories**, and **Save product**. See
[Products and variants](/gostore/docs/products/).

## Change or delete one

Open **Categories** and click **Edit**. Renaming changes what shoppers see
straight away.

**Delete category** removes the category and nothing else: its products stay in
the shop, simply no longer in that category. The page tells you how many
products it was taken off.
